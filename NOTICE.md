# Project information

Agent Workbench is a personal AI workspace developed by BStronger1.

The project is distributed under the MIT license. Third-party dependencies retain their respective licenses.

Claims about implemented capabilities and measured results are limited to the recorded tests. Demo fixtures are deterministic; they do not measure LLM quality or establish memory-retrieval improvements. A six-task real API probe using DMXAPI-deepseek-v4-flash accepted four tasks and retained two failed repair cases; see docs/LIVE-RESULTS.md. These development probes do not establish general model quality.
